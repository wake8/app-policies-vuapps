Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VU Share ("VU Share", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the VU Share Android application ("App"). VU Share is designed to let users share files directly between nearby devices.

At a glance: VU Share is designed for local, peer-to-peer file transfers. The App is not intended to upload your shared files to VU Share cloud storage. Crash reports are not automatically emailed; if the crash-report feature is available, you decide whether to share a report using your email application.

1. Information the App may access or store

The information involved depends on the features you use, your Android version, the permissions you grant and the libraries included in the installed version.

  • Files and media you select: The App accesses files you choose through Android's file picker, media picker or other permitted storage interfaces so it can send or receive the files you request.
  • Nearby-device and connection information: Device nickname and avatar, temporary connection identifiers, connection status and technical network information may be processed to discover, pair with and connect to nearby devices.
  • Transfer records: If transfer history is enabled, the App may store information such as file names, file sizes, timestamps, transfer status and the other device's nickname.
  • App preferences: Theme, home-screen visibility, receiving-folder and other settings may be stored locally on your device.
  • Crash diagnostics: If crash reporting is implemented and a crash occurs, diagnostic details such as the exception type and message, stack trace, crash time, App version, Android version and device manufacturer/model may be saved locally.

2. How file transfers work

VU Share is designed for local peer-to-peer sharing. When you start a sharing session, the App uses supported Android connectivity technologies to discover nearby devices and establish a connection. The transport used can depend on the devices, Android versions and available connection methods.

Files are sent to the recipient you select through the App's transfer flow. Incoming transfers should be accepted by the receiving user. VU Share does not provide cloud storage for shared files. Only share files with devices and people you trust.

3. How information is used

Information accessed or stored by the App is used to operate the features you request, including to:

  • Discover, pair with and connect nearby devices.
  • Send, receive and verify files.
  • Display transfer progress and, when enabled, transfer history.
  • Remember your preferences.
  • Diagnose errors when you choose to share a crash report with the developer.

4. Local storage, retention and deletion

App preferences, transfer history and pending crash reports are intended to be stored locally on your device. You can use the App's controls, where available, to hide or clear history and to discard a pending crash report.

Clearing transfer history removes the history records; it does not necessarily delete the actual files received on your device. Received files must be deleted separately using the relevant file-management controls. Files you receive are stored in the destination selected or configured in the App.

Local information may also be removed if you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, subject to Android storage behavior and any files you saved outside the App's private storage.

5. Crash reports and email

VU Share does not automatically email crash reports. If the App provides a crash-report option, diagnostic information may first be saved locally. You can choose whether to open an email application with a report addressed to vunnam888@gmail.com.

You can review, edit or cancel the email before sending it. Sending requires an email application and may require an internet connection. Once you send the email, the email provider's terms and privacy policy apply to the message. Do not send information you do not want to disclose.

6. Third-party services and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The App is designed not to upload shared files to VU Share servers. Android system services and connectivity components may process technical information needed for device discovery and communication. If you choose to send a crash report, the email application and email provider you use will process that message.

Any third-party SDK or library included in the released App may have its own data practices. The developer must review those components and ensure that this Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration accurately describe the released build.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Android version and connection method, VU Share may request nearby Wi-Fi or Bluetooth-related permissions, notification permission, access to files or media you select, and camera permission if you use QR-code scanning. Permissions should be requested when the relevant feature needs them.

You can review or revoke permissions in Android settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

8. Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards for local transfers and locally stored diagnostic information. No electronic transfer or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected, review connection requests and only accept transfers from devices you recognize.

9. Children's privacy

VU Share is a general-purpose file-sharing utility and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer through a support email, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Your choices

  • Choose which files to share and which incoming transfers to accept.
  • Manage the permissions granted to the App.
  • Control transfer-history visibility and clear locally stored history using available App settings.
  • Decline or discard a pending crash report instead of emailing it.
  • Contact the developer with privacy questions or requests.

11. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the App's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will be published at the location where this Policy is hosted. Please review it periodically.
